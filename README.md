## 📌 Project Overview

The **Global Super Store Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI Business Intelligence project designed to analyze retail sales performance across multiple regions, markets, product categories, customers, and time periods.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales, profit, quantity, customer activity, product performance, and regional business trends.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end Business Intelligence workflow, including data preparation, transformation, data modeling, KPI development, interactive visualization, and business analysis.

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Business Purpose |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | Measures overall revenue generated |
| Total Profit | Measures overall profitability |
| Total Quantity | Measures product sales volume |
| Total Orders | Measures order activity |
| Average Order Value | Measures average revenue generated per order |
| Profit Margin | Evaluates profitability relative to sales |
| Product Sales | Measures product-level revenue contribution |
| Regional Sales | Compares sales across geographic regions |
| Regional Profit | Compares profitability across regions |
| Customer Sales | Measures customer contribution to revenue |
